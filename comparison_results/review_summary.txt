Review Summary
==============

This summary is based on the saved result artifacts present in the original folder and does not claim any rerun beyond those files.

Why VGG16 is the strongest overall choice from these saved results:
- VGG16 shows the best convergence behavior across the recorded epochs.
- VGG16 reaches high validation accuracy earlier than the other models.
- VGG16 has the lowest validation loss among the three models, which indicates more stable generalization.
- VGG16 maintains strong accuracy with a very small train/validation gap in the saved run.

Observed strengths by model:
- VGG16: best overall convergence consistency, best mean validation accuracy, best validation-loss profile.
- ResNet50: strongest final classification report accuracy in the saved report, but weaker loss profile and less stable early learning.
- MobileNetV2: reasonable and efficient performance, but slower convergence than VGG16 in the saved history.

Main conclusion:
- If model selection is based on convergence speed, stability, and validation-loss behavior, VGG16 is the most defensible winner from the saved artifacts in this folder.
